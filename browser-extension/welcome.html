One more step: install Earful

Earful Bridge shows every browser tab that plays music or video as its own player in Earful, a media menu in the GNOME top bar. Earful isn't on this computer yet.

1. Install Earful

Open a terminal and run:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NGdev2/earful/main/install.sh | sh

No sudo needed. Earful works on Linux with GNOME 46 or newer.

2. Restart your computer

Then play something in a tab: it shows up in Earful's menu with its own play, pause and volume.

Already installed?

  • In Earful's settings, make sure Browser tabs is on.
  • Firefox from Snap asks once whether it may start Earful. Choose Allow. If you chose otherwise, click Ask again in Earful's settings.
  • Browsers installed as Flatpak or the Snap version of Chromium can't start Earful's helper.
