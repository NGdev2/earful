# Privacy

Earful and the Earful Bridge browser add-on collect no data and send nothing
anywhere.

- **What the add-on reads:** only what pages publish about the media they
  play: title, artist, album, cover image address, site name, whether it
  plays, position, length and volume. It never reads page content, form
  fields, cookies or history. Pages without media send nothing.
- **Where it goes:** to Earful's small helper program on your own computer,
  which shows each tab as a media player to your desktop (over D-Bus, which
  never leaves the computer).
- **No network, no tracking:** no analytics, no accounts, no servers. The only
  network traffic is loading cover art from the address a page or player
  provides, as the page itself would.
- **Settings** stay in GNOME's settings on your computer.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/NGdev2/earful/issues).
