# Ideas

Things that may come to Earful. Nothing here is promised. If one of them matters to you, or you have another idea, say so in an [issue](https://github.com/NGdev2/earful/issues).

## Next

- **Popups anywhere.** Drag a popup to any spot on the screen, with snapping to the edges and the center, instead of the six fixed positions ([#5](https://github.com/NGdev2/earful/issues/5)).

## Being tried

- **Real sound levels.** A meter that moves with what a player actually sounds like, not just its volume setting. This would bring back the *studio fader* volume style, which is hidden until its meter means something.

## Maybe later

- **LIVE and AD badges.** Show that a tab is a live stream or playing an ad. Players don't say this today, so the browser add-on would have to tell Earful.
- **An ignore list for autoplay noise.** Muted video previews and other sounds you never asked for would stay out of the menu.
